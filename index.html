Swing, seen from the bowler's end

Change the speed, seam angle, release line and which side of the ball is rough, and watch where it goes. The yellow line is the real flight; the dashed white line is where it would go with no swing at all. The ball knocks the stumps over only when your line and swing add up to a hit.

Tip: start it outside the line and let the swing bring it back onto the stumps, or aim straight at them.
